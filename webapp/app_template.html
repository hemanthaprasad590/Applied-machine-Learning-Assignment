About the modelTest period: 2025 – May 2026

Algorithm. CatBoost gradient boosting with a Poisson loss for count data. It has 1,201 shallow trees and was tuned on 2024 data. It runs entirely in this page.

Inputs. Cases in the last four weeks, 4- and 8-week averages, cases in the same week last year, national cases, rainfall, temperature and humidity over the last few weeks, week of the year, and the district's usual dengue level.

Data. Weekly district case counts from the Epidemiology Unit, Ministry of Health, via the denguedatahub package. Daily weather from NASA POWER, summed to the same weeks.

Use with care. Reported cases depend on testing and reporting, which change over time. Weather is a coarse grid estimate. Treat this as an early-warning aid alongside field surveillance.
